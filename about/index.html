About

Bio

I'm Zak Knudsen, a hands-on solutions architect in Barcelona. I have built for the web since 2014. Before that I built servers and Wi-Fi radios in Salt Lake City. I worked in Apple Stores in Honolulu and New York and became a Lead Specialist. I ran IT for New York creative agencies.

I spent six years as technical director at a Hong Kong digital agency. Then I joined Browser London, a UX and product agency, as tech lead. I was one of the first two staff in its Barcelona office. Since 2023 I have worked with Browser as a contract senior engineer.

I like the part of the job where a design has to survive production. I write decisions down as ADRs. I write procedures down as runbooks. I would rather say "I maintained it" than claim I built it.

How I work

  • Discovery first. I run it with client CTOs and product owners, and I recommend buying when building is not worth it.
  • Decisions in writing. ADRs for design choices. Runbooks for anything run under pressure.
  • Safe change. UAT before production. Manual production gates. Rollback planned before cutover.
  • AI-assisted, with guardrails. I use AI coding agents under written rules I set.
